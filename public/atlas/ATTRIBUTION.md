# Phoenix Atlas anatomy data: attribution and licence

The files in this folder (`skin.bin`, `muscular.bin`, `skeletal.bin`, `nerves.bin`, `vascular.bin`,
`organs.bin`) and the structure list in `src/anatomy/catalog.json` are adapted from the
**Svitylo 3D Anatomy Atlas data release 1.1.0** (`@authorod/svitylo-3d-anatomy-data`, economy
quality). They are shared under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
as the ShareAlike terms of the sources require.

The Svitylo release adapted these sources (modifiers evaluated, geometry corrections declared in its
`sources/geometry-fixes.json`, conversion to metres with Y up, simplification, kidneys and ear placed
into the Z-Anatomy body). Its `ATTRIBUTION.md` and `LICENSES.md` describe those changes in full; run
`npm run anatomy:fetch` to download them into `.cache/anatomy/`.

Not included, because their licences are unconfirmed or non-commercial: the cerebral cortex
surfaces (“Brainder”), the University of Washington white matter, the University of Dundee inner
ear and the Lissie Cowley kidney.

## Changes made by Phoenix Atlas

`scripts/build-atlas.ts` makes these changes, reproducibly from the checksummed release:

- **Selection.** Hair, bursae, tendon sheaths, muscle attachment patches, most fasciae (the
  iliotibial tract, plantar and palmar aponeuroses, epicranial aponeurosis, patellar retinacula,
  flexor retinaculum of the wrist and posterior thoracolumbar fascia are kept), joint capsules and
  synovial membranes, paranasal sinuses, body cavities, liver segments, internal kidney parts,
  segmental bronchi, meninges, ventricles and nuclei inside the brainstem are left out. One of the
  spinal cord's white matter meshes is kept and named “Spinal cord”; the fibrous capsule of each
  kidney is named “Kidney”.
- **Layers.** Structures are grouped into six layers: surface regions, muscles, bones and joints,
  nervous system, vessels and organs (viscera, heart and lymphoid organs).
- **Geometry.** Vertices are welded, faces are oriented so normals point outwards, each structure
  is simplified with meshoptimizer to an absolute error of 0.25–0.5 mm, and positions are quantised
  to 16 bits per axis within each layer's bounds.
- **Depth.** Muscles are marked deep when less than about 15% of sampled surface points can be seen
  from outside the body with only muscles and bones present.
- **Names.** Placeholder names in the source are replaced (“Unlabelled vessel segment”), and the
  side is added to display names.
- **Added.** The two cerebral hemispheres (`approx.cerebral_hemisphere_l/_r`) are not from the
  sources: they are an approximate shape generated by Phoenix Atlas and fitted inside the cranium,
  so the brain can still be tapped. They are labelled as approximate in the app.

## Status

The upstream release is on its `preview` channel: its licence audit is pending and no formal
anatomical review has been done. Phoenix Atlas is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device.
